## Description

Drinkly makes daily hydration feel simple, motivating, and beautifully personal.

Log water in one tap, see your progress at a glance, and build a routine that fits naturally into your day. Thoughtful reminders, clear statistics, rewarding milestones, and premium themes help you stay consistent without adding complexity.

KEY FEATURES

- One-tap water logging
- Personalized daily hydration goals
- Interactive Home Screen widget
- Smart reminders throughout the day
- Weekly, monthly, and yearly statistics
- Hydration history and progress insights
- Streaks and achievements
- Premium light and dark themes
- Milliliter-based tracking
- Privacy-friendly local storage

Drinkly is designed for wellness tracking and does not provide medical advice. Individual hydration needs vary.
